Statistics for Management

Book: Statistics for Management (BA4101, Anna University MBA Semester I, Regulation 2021) · Study guide derived from the full textbook, condensed unit-by-unit into notes, key terms, exam-prep summaries, and practice questions.

Unit 1

Probability and Probability Distributions

Basic concepts of probability, counting rules/sample space/events, theorems/conditional probability/Bayes' Theorem, random variables and probability distributions, Binomial and Poisson distributions, and the Normal distribution.
Unit 2

Sampling Distribution and Estimation

Introduction to sampling distributions, sampling distribution of mean/proportion and the Central Limit Theorem, sampling techniques, point/interval estimation for large samples, and interval estimation for small samples/determining sample size.
Unit 3

Testing of Hypothesis — Parametric Tests

Introduction to hypothesis testing, Z-tests for means/proportions (large samples), t-tests for means (small samples), the F-test for two sample standard deviations, One-Way ANOVA, and Two-Way ANOVA.
Unit 4

Non-Parametric Tests

Introduction to non-parametric tests and the sign test, the Mann-Whitney U Test, the Kruskal-Wallis Test, the Kolmogorov-Smirnov Test and One Sample Run Test, the Chi-Square test for goodness of fit/variance, and the Chi-Square test for independence of attributes.
Unit 5

Correlation and Regression

Correlation meaning/types/Karl Pearson's coefficient, the coefficient of determination and rank correlation, regression lines and the method of least squares, estimation of the regression line, and the standard error of estimate.

This is a condensed study guide, not a copy of the textbook — each topic distills the book's content into notes, key terms, an exam-prep summary, and click-to-reveal practice questions.
