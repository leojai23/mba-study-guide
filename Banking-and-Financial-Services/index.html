Banking and Financial Services

Book: Banking and Financial Services (BA4003, Anna University MBA Semester III) — Thakur Publication · Study guide derived from the full textbook, condensed unit-by-unit into notes, key terms, exam-prep summaries, and practice questions.

Unit 1

Introduction to Indian Banking System and Performance Evaluation

Overview & structure of Indian banking, commercial bank functions, co-operative banks/NABARD/payment systems, RBI & key regulations, bank financial statements, and the CAMELS rating system.
Unit 2

Managing Bank Funds/Products & Risk Management

Capital adequacy & deposits, loan and investment management, ALM, financial distress, all major bank risk types (interest rate, forex, credit, market, operational, solvency), NPAs, and bank mergers & acquisitions.
Unit 3

Development in Banking Technology

Payment & settlement systems (RTGS/NEFT), paper vs electronic payments, internet banking, plastic money & cards, e-money & ATMs, the IT Act 2000, RBI's digital payments vision, and e-banking security threats.
Unit 4

Asset Based Financial Services

NBFCs, Leasing (concepts, types & financial evaluation), Hire Purchase, Underwriting, and Mutual Funds (structure, types, and advantages/disadvantages).
Unit 5

Insurance and Other Fee Based Financial Services

Insurance (principles & types), the Insurance Act 1938, IRDA, Venture Capital Financing, Bills Discounting, Factoring, and Merchant Banking (functions/services & SEBI regulation).
Exam Prep

Case Studies & Exam Papers

7 solved case studies + 1 unsolved, plus two full Anna University exam papers (2016 & 2017) with every question linked to the matching topic in this app.
