Unit 4Data Analysis: Meaning and Types

Book: Business Research Methods (BA4205, Anna University MBA Sem II, Regulation 2021)

1. Overview

With clean, tabulated data in hand, the researcher moves to analysis. This topic surveys what data analysis involves, its objectives, its two broad types, and the role of statistical software in carrying it out.

2. Detailed Notes

2.1 Introduction to Data Analysis

Data analysis is the process of inspecting, cleaning, transforming and modelling data to extract useful information, draw conclusions, and support decision-making regarding the research objectives.

2.2 Objectives of Data Analysis

  • To summarise and describe the collected data meaningfully.
  • To identify patterns, relationships or differences among variables.
  • To test the hypotheses formulated at the start of the study.
  • To provide a sound basis for conclusions and managerial recommendations.

2.3 Types of Data Analysis

TypeDescription
Qualitative AnalysisInterprets non-numerical data (text, images, observations) to identify themes and patterns.
Quantitative AnalysisApplies statistical and mathematical techniques to numerical data.
Univariate AnalysisExamines one variable at a time (e.g. mean, frequency distribution).
Bivariate AnalysisExamines the relationship between two variables (e.g. correlation, cross-tabulation).
Multivariate AnalysisExamines relationships among three or more variables simultaneously.

2.4 Phases of Data Analysis

  1. Organising and cleaning the prepared data.
  2. Choosing the appropriate statistical technique based on the data type and research objective.
  3. Running the analysis, often using statistical software.
  4. Interpreting the output in the context of the research problem.
  5. Drawing conclusions and formulating recommendations.

2.5 Application of Statistical Software

Modern business research relies heavily on statistical software to perform the calculations involved in analysis quickly and accurately:

SoftwareDescription
SPSS (Statistical Package for the Social Sciences)Widely used, menu-driven software popular for survey and social-science style business research.
SAS (Statistical Analysis System)A powerful system used heavily in large-scale corporate and analytics environments.
STATAPopular in economics and econometrics-heavy research, combining a command language with menu-driven options.

3. Key Terms

The process of inspecting, cleaning, transforming and modelling data to extract useful information and support conclusions.
Univariate Analysis
Analysis that examines a single variable at a time.
Bivariate Analysis
Analysis that examines the relationship between two variables.
Multivariate Analysis
Analysis that examines relationships among three or more variables simultaneously.

4. Exam-Prep Summary

  • Data analysis objectives: summarise data, identify patterns/relationships, test hypotheses, support conclusions.
  • Types by data: qualitative (non-numerical) vs quantitative (numerical/statistical).
  • Types by variable count: univariate (1) → bivariate (2) → multivariate (3+).
  • Analysis phases: organise/clean → choose technique → run analysis → interpret → conclude.
  • Common software: SPSS (survey-focused), SAS (large-scale corporate analytics), STATA (econometrics).

5. Practice Questions

  1. What are the objectives of data analysis?
    Show model answer
    Data analysis aims to summarise and describe the collected data meaningfully, identify patterns and relationships or differences among variables, test the hypotheses formulated at the outset of the study, and provide a sound basis for conclusions and managerial recommendations.
  2. Distinguish between univariate, bivariate and multivariate analysis.
    Show model answer
    Univariate analysis examines a single variable at a time, such as its mean or frequency distribution. Bivariate analysis examines the relationship between exactly two variables, such as through correlation or cross-tabulation. Multivariate analysis examines the relationships among three or more variables simultaneously, such as through multiple regression or factor analysis.
  3. Name and briefly describe three statistical software packages used in business research.
    Show model answer
    SPSS (Statistical Package for the Social Sciences) is a widely used, menu-driven package popular for survey-based research. SAS (Statistical Analysis System) is a powerful system used in large-scale corporate and analytics environments. STATA is popular in economics and econometrics-heavy research, combining a command language with menu-driven options.
