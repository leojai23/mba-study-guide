Unit 5The Research Report

Book: Business Research Methods (BA4205, Anna University MBA Sem II, Regulation 2021)

1. Overview

A research study is only as useful as the report that communicates it. This topic covers what a research report is, what makes it good, the functions it serves, its main types, and why report writing matters.

2. Detailed Notes

1.1 Introduction to the Research Report

A research report is the formal written document that communicates the purpose, methodology, findings and conclusions of a research study to its intended audience, translating analysis into a form that can inform decisions.

1.2 Characteristics of a Good Research Report

  • Clear and precise in language, avoiding unnecessary jargon.
  • Logically organised, with a structure the reader can follow easily.
  • Accurate and objective, fairly representing the data and its limitations.
  • Complete, covering the full research process from problem to conclusion.
  • Concise, presenting information efficiently without padding.

1.3 Function of Reports

  • Communicates research findings to decision-makers who did not conduct the study themselves.
  • Provides a permanent record of the research for future reference.
  • Serves as the basis for managerial action and policy decisions.
  • Allows the research methodology to be reviewed and, if needed, replicated.

1.4 Types of Reports

TypeDescription
Technical ReportDetailed, methodologically thorough, written for researchers/specialists.
Popular/Management ReportSimplified, emphasises findings and recommendations, written for non-technical management readers.
Interim ReportA progress update issued before the study is complete.
Final ReportThe complete, conclusive report issued at the end of the study.
Oral ReportA verbal presentation of findings, often supported by a written summary and slides.

1.5 Contents of a Report

At a minimum, a report should cover: the title and purpose of the study, the research methodology used, the key findings, the conclusions drawn, and recommendations for action.

1.6 Importance of Report Writing

No matter how rigorous the underlying research, its value is realised only when it is clearly communicated to those who will act on it. A well-written report builds the credibility of the research, ensures findings are correctly understood, and provides a lasting, reviewable record that supports informed managerial decision-making.

3. Key Terms

Research Report
The formal written document that communicates the purpose, methodology, findings and conclusions of a research study.
Technical Report
A detailed, methodologically thorough report written for researchers or specialists.
Popular/Management Report
A simplified report emphasising findings and recommendations, written for non-technical management readers.

4. Exam-Prep Summary

  • Research report = formal document communicating purpose, method, findings, conclusions.
  • Good report: clear, logically organised, accurate/objective, complete, concise.
  • Report types: technical vs popular/management; interim vs final; written vs oral.
  • Minimum contents: title/purpose, methodology, findings, conclusions, recommendations.

5. Practice Questions

  1. What are the characteristics of a good research report?
    Show model answer
    A good research report is clear and precise in language, logically organised so the reader can follow it easily, accurate and objective in representing the data and its limitations, complete in covering the full research process, and concise in presenting information without unnecessary padding.
  2. Differentiate between a technical report and a popular (management) report.
    Show model answer
    A technical report is detailed and methodologically thorough, written for researchers or specialists who need to evaluate the methods used. A popular or management report is simplified, emphasising findings and recommendations over methodological detail, and is written for non-technical management readers who need to act on the results.
  3. Why is report writing important in the research process?
    Show model answer
    Report writing is important because the value of research is only realised once it is clearly communicated to decision-makers. A well-written report builds the credibility of the research, ensures findings are correctly understood, and provides a lasting, reviewable record that supports informed managerial action.
